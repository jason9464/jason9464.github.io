:page_facing_up: A new preprint *"Long Live the Librarian! A Persistent Search Sub-Agent for Energy-Efficient Multi-Agent Software Engineering Systems"* is now on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27787). We introduce the Librarian, a persistent search sub-agent that reduces redundant retrieval and lowers the energy cost of MAS.
